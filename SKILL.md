# Personal Agent Glymphatic Clearance Aquaporin-4 Sleep Skill

Designed for **OpenClaw**, **Hermes**, and autonomous personal AI assistants to model cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) and interstitial fluid (ISF) bulk convective exchange, quantify the +64% interstitial space expansion during N3 Slow-Wave Sleep (SWS), evaluate sleep posture coefficients (lateral vs supine vs prone), and assess Aquaporin-4 (AQP4) water channel polarization.

## Agent In-Context Capabilities

- **Glymphatic Convective Modeling**: Computes the Neuro-Metabolic Solute Clearance Score (NMSCS) based on slow-wave sleep duration and nocturnal noradrenergic withdrawal.
- **Biomechanical Posture Coefficients**: Applies peer-reviewed empirical multipliers (1.25x for lateral right, 0.85x for supine, 0.70x for prone) reflecting cervical jugular drainage dynamics.
- **Toxicological Disruption Auditing**: Models the blunting effect of evening alcohol and sedatives on AQP4 channel polarization and delta-wave amplitude.

## Tools Summary

1. `model_nocturnal_glymphatic_flux`: Computes NMSCS score, interstitial expansion, and A-beta/tau clearance tier.
2. `prescribe_glymphatic_optimization_routine`: Tailors sleep position, ambient bedroom temperature, and pre-sleep protocols to maximize convective flux.
